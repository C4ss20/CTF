# Evidence & attribution policy

## What to preserve

- A short sanitized command or snippet that a reader can understand.
- Minimal observed output linked to a precise conclusion.
- Relevant screenshots labeled with what they demonstrate.
- Both the positive result and any important negative/retest results.
- The creator, event, and video link for third-party educational sources.

## What NOT to publish

- Passwords, access tokens, cookies, API keys, private keys, personal data, or client information.
- Unredacted packet captures or scan results that expose private targets.
- Active competition flags or challenge materials when publication is restricted.
- Third-party code, screenshots, video transcript, or challenge assets without appropriate permission.

## Claim-to-evidence ledger

| Claim | Evidence | Verification status |
|---|---|---|
| Example only (not a finding) | Provide a local sanitized artifact when available | Unverified |

Create an `evidence/` directory under each verified challenge when evidence exists. Name files sequentially, e.g. `01-initial-observation.png`, and reference them in the walkthrough. Attribute any learning resources directly in the challenge's References section.

An AI-generated example is not evidence of a completed test.
